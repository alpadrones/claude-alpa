The Platform

One System.
Every Mission.

A standardized, modular control platform that adapts to any mission objective. Same interface, same cradle, different capability — in seconds.

The Concept

Standardized by Design.
Versatile by Nature.

The ALPA Platform is the core of everything we build. Instead of developing isolated systems for each mission, we created a single standardized interface that accepts interchangeable mission modules.

One operator. One set of controls. One universal cradle. Swap the mission box, and the entire system transforms — from reconnaissance to precision strike to persistent surveillance.

Components

What Makes Up
the Platform

Four components. One unified system. Each designed for maximum interoperability and minimum complexity.

VR Goggles

Immersive first-person view with real-time HD feed and heads-up mission data overlay.

Controller

Ergonomic remote with tactile feedback. Designed for gloved operation in any conditions.

Universal Cradle

Standardized mounting that accepts any ALPA mission box. Vehicle or ground deployed.

Mission Box

Interchangeable payload module. Each box contains a complete mission-ready system. Swap in seconds.

User Experience

Zero Training.
Full Capability.

The platform was designed around the operator — not the other way around. VR goggles provide an immersive, intuitive first-person experience that feels natural from the first use.

Any soldier who can use a gaming controller can operate the ALPA Platform. The interface remains identical regardless of which mission module is loaded — learn once, operate everything.

Technology

Mission-Configurable
by Design

Every ALPA drone is fully customizable across four key dimensions — communications, payload, attack strategy, and power. Configure the exact system your mission demands.

The Approach

Four Axes of
Customization

No two missions are the same. That's why every ALPA system is built around four interchangeable modules — each with multiple configurations that can be combined to create the exact drone your operation needs.

Communications. Payload. Attack strategy. Power. Mix and match across all four to produce a system perfectly tailored to the threat, terrain, and objective.

Module 01

Communications

Three distinct data link options to match the electronic environment, range requirements, and jamming threat level of each mission.

01

Fiber-Optic Link

Physical cable delivers zero-latency HD video and telemetry with complete immunity to RF jamming. Ideal for high-EW contested environments where signal integrity is critical.

5G Cellular Network

Leverages existing 5G infrastructure for extended range operations with high bandwidth. Best for urban environments and areas with cellular coverage.

Directional Radio Antenna

Narrowly focused radio beam that continuously tracks the drone, reducing interception probability. Balances range and resilience without physical tethering.

Module 02

Payload & Weapons

Multiple warhead and weapon configurations to match the target type — from personnel to hardened fortifications.

02

Anti-Personnel Explosive

Fragmentation warhead optimized for soft targets in open or semi-covered positions.

Anti-Vehicle Charge

Shaped charge designed to penetrate armored vehicles and light AFVs.

Anti-Fortification

High-explosive warhead for bunkers, reinforced structures, and defensive positions.

Mortar Revolver

Rotary mortar cartridge system for multiple consecutive drops from altitude over a target area.

Mounted Weapon

Lightweight weapon mounted on the drone for reusable, sustained engagement capability.

Module 03

Attack Strategy

Different flight and approach profiles to defeat air defenses, avoid trajectory prediction, and overwhelm countermeasures.

03

Solo — Direct Path

Single drone with direct trajectory to target. Maximum simplicity and speed. Best for undefended or time-critical targets with minimal air defense presence.

Solo — Random Path

Single drone with randomized, unpredictable trajectory changes. Prevents enemy systems from estimating flight path and computing intercept solutions.

Swarm — Guide + Followers

Lead drone sets the trajectory while multiple follower drones replicate the path. Saturates air defenses by overwhelming tracking and intercept capacity with volume.

Module 04

Power & Endurance

Energy configuration determines mission duration, range, and weight. From lightweight sprint batteries to unlimited tethered power.

04

Small Battery

Minimum weight, maximum speed. Short-range sprint missions where agility and portability are the priority.

Medium Battery

Standard configuration balancing endurance and payload capacity. Covers the majority of operational scenarios.

Large Battery

Maximum onboard energy for deep penetration and extended loitering. Increased weight traded for longer time on station.

Tethered Power Cable

Direct power via copper cable from ground vehicle or position. Unlimited endurance for persistent surveillance and sustained operations.
